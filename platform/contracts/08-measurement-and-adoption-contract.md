# C08 Measurement And Adoption Contract

## Purpose

Tie CO OS work to Malik's four 90-day metrics.

## Metrics

1. Recover agency effort through agent-handled workflows.
2. Improve margin.
3. Put a productised AI offer in pilot by end of Q3 2026.
4. Reach 50 percent weekly staff usage.

## Draft Rules

### Rule 1: Measurement Does Not Equal Enforcement

Enforcement: hard-enforced in release language and reporting review.

Mechanism: every metric must state whether it measures or gates.

Evidence: weekly report distinguishes measurement from enforcement.

### Rule 2: Adoption Is A First-Class Metric

Enforcement: soft/model-honoured until staff usage source is available.

Mechanism: weekly usage readout.

Evidence: gateway audit logs and Claude analytics where available.

## Open Questions

- What is the baseline effort cost?
- Which workflows count as recovered effort?
- What usage source is reliable enough for weekly reporting?
