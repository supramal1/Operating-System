# C10 Vendor And Commercial Contract

## Purpose

Track external dependencies and commercial gates that affect CO OS decisions.

## Draft Rules

### Rule 1: Vendor Guarantees Need Evidence

Enforcement: soft/model-honoured initially

Mechanism: decision register requiring source evidence.

Evidence: terms, docs, quote, contract, or tested capability.

### Rule 2: Open Decisions Stay Open

Enforcement: hard-enforced in planning documents

Mechanism: open-decision labels and review gate.

Evidence: decision register shows status and evidence.
